01 · About Me

More than a title.
A journey.

I’m Rahel Malik, an educator, private tutor, and trainer from Fenchuganj, Sylhet. Teaching was never simply a title I wanted to carry; it gradually became one of the most meaningful parts of my everyday life.

My experience has grown through coaching centers, private tutoring, independent student batches, and specialized training. I have worked with students across different subjects and different learning needs, from academic subjects such as Science, Accounting, Finance, ICT, and English to Spoken English, IELTS, Grammar, and basic computer training.

One of the things I value most is communication. I enjoy talking with people, understanding them, encouraging them, and helping students become more confident. Over time, the love and respect I have received from students has become one of the most meaningful rewards of teaching.
